How it works

Review every change before writing

  1. 1

    Scan the archive

    Read JPEG EXIF, filename clocks, filesystem time, camera make, and model.

    capture-clock-repair scan ./archive
  2. 2

    Review the plan

    Check trusted dates, filename proposals, weak fallbacks, and timezone conflicts in CSV.

    open clock-review/review.csv
  3. 3

    Apply or undo

    Create adjacent XMP sidecars for approved rows. Remove unchanged sidecars with the undo manifest.

    capture-clock-repair apply …

Scope and privacy

Know what the CLI reads and writes

The CLI reads local JPEG metadata and filenames. It does not upload photos or collect telemetry.

It reports RAW, HEIC, video, TIFF, and PNG files as unsupported without changing them.

Proposals are evidence for review, not proof of when a photo was taken.

  • Trusted EXIF is checked again during apply.
  • Each inferred date includes its source and confidence.
  • Each write has a checksummed undo record.
